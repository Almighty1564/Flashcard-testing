Manual position

The level crosshair uses phone tilt, not ground slope. Nearby metal can distort a steady compass. Unknown or excessive compass uncertainty never turns green.

GPS elevation is WGS84 ellipsoid height, not terrain height or guaranteed mean sea level. Missing altitude is shown as unavailable. Dish elevation is a separate angle. Feet/meters switch: tap the elevation.
