How is the phone mounted?

Lay the phone flat, screen up, with its physical top/camera edge pointing in the antenna’s direction. The phone must rotate with the antenna.

Offsets & accuracy settings

Antenna angle = phone angle + offset. Use a known mounting/reflector correction. Offset dishes do not necessarily point perpendicular to the dish face. These offsets do not repair magnetic interference.

Azimuth turns green only when reported compass uncertainty is no larger than your zone. Unknown accuracy stays amber. The zone is not the antenna’s beamwidth.

Placement, troubleshooting & calculation sources

Azimuth first. Elevation second.

For base mode, the phone must sit on the part that rotates, not the stationary tripod. Use the physical top/camera edge, regardless of screen rotation. For elevation, move it onto a surface that tilts with the antenna and enter the correct angle offset. “Both” requires a mount that follows both axes.

Keep metal away from the compass.

Steel, magnets, magnetic cases and nearby electronics can cause stable but wrong headings. Calibrate away from the antenna, then check whether bringing it closer changes the bearing. True north conversion cannot remove this error. A nonmagnetic standoff may help.

No compass or no green?

Use HTTPS and allow location and motion/orientation when asked. If your browser blocks sensors, try Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. A desktop can calculate targets with manual coordinates, but has no phone compass. Missing, stale, uncalibrated or unknown-accuracy readings cannot produce a green azimuth.

Limits & sources

GEO equatorial slots only. Not a tracker for Starlink, moving/inclined satellites or MEO. Horizon calculations do not detect trees, buildings, terrain, coverage or an active transponder. Altitude is WGS84 ellipsoid height when GPS provides it; otherwise 0 km.

WGS84 topocentric geometry; NOAA/BGS WMM2025, valid through 2029. East declination is positive: true = magnetic + declination. Presets checked 21 Sep 2026 against the SES fleet map and Eutelsat. Nominal positions can change; use your provider’s assigned position. Coordinates and custom satellites are kept in this tab’s memory only.
